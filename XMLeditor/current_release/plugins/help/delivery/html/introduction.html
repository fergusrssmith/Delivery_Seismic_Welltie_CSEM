Introduction to delivery Editor.

The delivery editor enables you to assemble valid XML to drive the inversions performed by delivery. There are about 6 main sections in the XML, and the next  few sections of help give you a rough feel of what these sections control, and the required inputs.


It is very useful to read or peruse the
Computers and Geosciences paper/preprint describing delivery at
